Genre Packs

A genre pack is a curated subset of the catalog, in the order a new project of that genre meets the presets.

A pack is a starting shelf, not a lock-in — every name in a pack is a normal catalog preset you can also play on its own, retune, or ignore.

The packs also appear on their own shelf in the JuiceWorks window, with their preset counts and one-line promises.

Platformer Pack — 18 presets

“Jump, land, bounce, collect: the full movement vocabulary of a platformer, tuned springy.”

JumpSquashCharge, JumpStretch, DoubleJumpPop, WallJumpPush, LandSoft, LandHard, BouncePadLaunch, CrouchSquash, DashStretch, PickupCoin, PickupStar, PickupHeart, HitLight, DeathSquash, RespawnPop, CameraLandThud, IdleBreathe, UiButtonPress.

Shooter Pack — 18 entries

“Recoil, muzzle, clang and crit: gunplay feel from the first pistol to the cannon.”

ShootRecoilSmall, ShootRecoilBig, ShotgunPump, MuzzlePop, ReloadDip, CannonBlast, HitLight, HitMedium, HitHeavy, HitCrit, HitMetal, LastHitSlowmo, AmmoPickup, ExplosiveSmall, CameraShakeSmall, CameraShakeMedium, CameraKickUp — with ExplosiveSmall also closing the list in the authored data.

Runner Pack — 16 presets

“Coins that sparkle, slides that scrape, near misses that jolt: forward motion, celebrated.”

RunPuff, SlideScrape, JumpStretch, LandSoft, PickupCoin, CoinSparkleBurst, PickupGem, PickupPowerup, BodyBump, CameraNudgeLeft, CameraNudgeRight, CameraHitJolt, ScorePopupRise, UiCounterBump, DeathPop, HeartbeatThump.

Using a pack

Start a new project by walking the pack top to bottom: attach a JuicePlayer where the pack's first presets belong (movement first for a platformer, recoil and hits first for a shooter), preview each preset on your own sprites from the window, and copy the ones that fit into your own preset assets for tuning. Nothing in a pack needs to be adopted as a set.
