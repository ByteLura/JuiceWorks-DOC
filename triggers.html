Triggers

JuiceTrigger is the zero-code playback surface.

It fires a preset — or a sequence — on an event, on this object or another one. Put it on a button, a pickup, or an enemy, pick the moment and the preset, and the wiring is done. No manager object and no script are needed; the trigger finds the JuicePlayer on its target or adds one when a preset is set.

The seven event kinds

  • Manual — fires only when Fire() is called, from code or a UnityEvent.
  • OnAwake — fires once when the component wakes up.
  • OnEnable — fires every time the component is enabled.
  • PointerDown — fires on pointer down, for UI elements and physics raycast targets.
  • CollisionEnter2D — fires on a 2D collision enter.
  • TriggerEnter2D — fires on a 2D trigger enter.
  • OnBecameVisible — fires when the object becomes visible to a camera.

Fields

  • Player — the JuicePlayer to play on. Defaults to a JuicePlayer on this object.
  • Preset — your own preset asset to play (optional).
  • Preset name — a built-in catalog preset to play, by name (optional; used when no preset asset is set).
  • Sequencer — a sequencer to play instead of a single preset (optional; takes priority over the preset fields).
  • Delay — seconds to wait before playing after the trigger fires.
  • Fire once — fire once, then ignore later events until ResetTrigger() is called.

Fire() and ResetTrigger() are public, so buttons and other UnityEvents can drive a trigger directly.

Typical placements

  • A UI button: PointerDown + UiButtonPress.
  • An enemy: CollisionEnter2D + a hit preset from the Hits & Impacts shelf.
  • A pickup: TriggerEnter2D + its Pickup preset, with fire-once set.
  • A spawner or entrance: OnEnable + a Transition preset, or a sequence for a staged entrance.
