Sample Guide: Juice Arcade

A tiny 2D tap-target arcade that exists for one reason: to let you feel the presets.

Targets pop up, you tap them, they react — squash, flash, shake, score popup — and every reaction is a built-in preset played by name. The game scripts do bookkeeping only; the feel is all data.

What ships, and what does not

The sample ships as scripts only, plus this build guide. No serialized scene or prefab ships in the package — deliberately: a scene you assemble from scripts holds references you made yourself, while a shipped serialized scene is the classic source of missing-script breakage in a buyer's project. The build below takes about two minutes.

The four scripts, in Samples~/Demo (imported as Assets/Samples/JuiceWorks Pro/1.0.0/Juice Arcade):

  • JuiceArcade — the target pool and spawn timer, plus the score.
  • JuiceTapTarget — tap handling: a tap plays a preset and spawns the popup.
  • JuiceScorePopup — the rising popup that hides itself after its rise.
  • JuiceHud — score display, feel-test buttons, and a code-built reward chain played through the sequencer.

The sample's assembly references the Runtime only, and every call it makes is a public call your own game can make.

Build the scene (about two minutes)

In an empty scene:

  1. Create a SpriteRenderer (any sprite), add a CircleCollider2D, a JuicePlayer, and JuiceTapTarget. Save it as a prefab named TapTarget — this is the target.
  2. Create an empty object named JuiceArcade with the JuiceArcade component. Set its Target Prefab to the prefab.
  3. Add a Canvas with a Text for the score and a JuiceHud component; set Score Label. Add four Buttons wired to the HUD's PlayHeavyHit, PlayPickup, PlayShake, PlayRewardChain methods, and one to PlayHaptic.
  4. Create a popup: a Canvas Text with JuiceScorePopup plus a JuicePlayer, its Label set. Assign it to the arcade's Popup, and the HUD to its Hud.
  5. Add a camera at z = -10 looking at the origin. Press Play.

The sample uses plain uGUI Text throughout — the package has no TextMeshPro dependency.

What to try

  • Tap targets: each tap plays HitMedium and a rising score popup.
  • The HUD buttons fire a heavy hit, a coin pickup, and a shake on the score label, then a three-step reward chain through the sequencer.
  • Preview on the targets: open Tools > ByteLura > JuiceWorks Pro, select any target, and preview other presets on it from the browser — try HitSlime, DeathSpinOut, and LastHitSlowmo.
  • On an Android device, taps and buttons buzz with their preset patterns. In the Editor and on desktop the haptics backend is silent by design (Haptics).
